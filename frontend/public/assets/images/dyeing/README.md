# Traditional Bodo Botanical Dyeing Assets

Directory for photographs related to traditional Bodo handloom dyeing:
- Undyed yarn and raw threads
- Yellow turmeric-dyed yarn
- Natural red and reddish-yellow dyes
- Indigo and blue-dyed yarn
- Green and black dyed threads
- Natural dye sources such as bark, leaves, and roots
- Alum (Fitkaari) and other documented mordants
- Traditional dyeing equipment, vessels, and processes
- Drying yarn and finished dyed skeins
